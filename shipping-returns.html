Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping on US orders over $99 (3 to 6 business days).
  • Standard shipping under $99: $7.95.
  • Expedited (2 business days): $18. Overnight: $32.
  • Orders placed before 1 PM PT ship the same business day from our Bend, OR warehouse.
  • We ship to Canada. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout.

Returns & Exchanges

Not the right fit? Return unworn items within 60 days for a full refund or free exchange. Start a return from your account or with your order number and email. Return shipping is free for US orders.

Lifetime Warranty

If a Cairnwood product fails due to a defect in materials or workmanship, we will repair it, replace it or refund it for the life of the product. Normal wear and tear can be repaired for a small fee through our repair program.

Questions?

Email hello@cairnwoodoutfitters.com or text HELP to our customer care line. We answer Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM PT.
