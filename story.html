Our Story

Cairnwood started in 2011 in a one-car garage in Bend, Oregon, with a sewing machine, a stack of waxed canvas and a simple idea: outdoor gear should last long enough to collect stories.

Today we are a team of designers, guides and gear nerds who still test every product on the trails around the Three Sisters, Smith Rock and the Deschutes River. Our flagship store sits on Bond Street downtown, and we ship to adventurers in all 50 states and Canada.

Stewardship

We give 1% of every sale to local trail and watershed organizations, use recycled or organic fabrics in over 70% of our line and run a repair program that has kept more than 40,000 garments in use. Wear it out, send it back, and we will fix it.

Thanks for being part of the Cairnwood Crew. We will see you out there.
